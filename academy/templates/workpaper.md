## 1. Risk scenario
Describe asset, actor/source, weakness, trust boundary, attack path and impact.

## 2. Control objective
What must the control prevent, detect or allow the organization to recover from?

## 3. Control under test
Describe the current mechanism and who can change or bypass it.

## 4. Scope / population
Document the relevant repository, branch, workflow, PR population, configuration export or event period.

## 5. Audit procedure
Include both normal and negative tests.

## 6. Evidence
List exact references: commit SHA, PR, workflow run, ruleset export, log, screenshot/export, file path, artifact digest, etc.

## 7. Result
Separate observation from interpretation.

## 8. Exceptions / limitations
Document unavailable evidence, incomplete population, contradictory evidence and scope limitations.

## 9. Residual risk
Explain exposure, impact, bypass potential, detectability and recoverability after the current control level.

## 10. Additional control required?
If residual risk is not acceptable, specify the next control level and why.

## 11. Re-test
Document the result after remediation or strengthening.

## 12. Conclusion
State only what the evidence supports. Include remaining residual risk and limitations.
